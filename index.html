Questions

Good to know

I'm not flexible and I've never done yoga. Is this for me?

Yes — that's the norm here, not the exception. Yoga therapy meets you at your current mobility and builds from there. Nothing requires you to touch your toes.

I have type 2 diabetes / high blood pressure / a disc issue. Can I still take part?

Usually yes, with the right adjustments. The free consultation covers your medical history, and for anything significant I'll ask you to check with your doctor and, where helpful, coordinate with them. Yoga therapy complements medical treatment; it doesn't replace it.

How much time does it take each week?

A typical plan is one session together plus a short home routine (often 10–20 minutes) a few times a week, along with quick desk resets. It's designed to survive a busy week.

Is it online or in person?

Both are available. Many clients do the consultation and regular sessions online, and some prefer in person for the first few. We'll pick what suits you.

Will this cure my back pain / reverse my diabetes?

No responsible practitioner can promise that. What yoga therapy can do is reduce pain and stiffness for many people, build a sustainable movement habit, and support the lifestyle changes your healthcare team recommends. Results vary from person to person.

Health disclaimer: Realign Yoga Therapy provides complementary movement and wellbeing support. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and does not replace care from a qualified healthcare professional. Consult your doctor before starting any new exercise programme, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication that affects blood sugar or blood pressure. If you experience chest pain, dizziness, or other concerning symptoms, stop and seek medical help.
